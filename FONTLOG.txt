FONTLOG for Moira Astro
======================

Project: original astrology symbol font; regular weight.
The master inventory has been covered through Batch 21. Coverage refers to
the selected inventory, not every symbol or historical convention ever used.

2026-11-09  0.14.0-alpha.1 / internal revision 0.950
  Integrated Batch 23: twenty aspect multiples, reached by explicit [astro:*]
  discretionary tokens only. No Unicode mappings were added or changed.
  Added raised figures 5, 7 and 9 to the existing 2, 3 and 4 notation.
  Preserved all 726 earlier glyphs, 225 mappings and 711 optional tokens.
