# Moira Astro - accepted direction and current proposals

23 October 2026

## Accepted by the owner

The owner's instruction for this batch is: keep the **letter family as the minor-aspect default, with the alternatives retained**.

This records the direction from Batch 02 as the selected direction for future font work. Its thirteen letter-based longitude forms are Q, Q², S, S², S³, N, N², N⁴, D, D³, U, Qd and V. Regional and geometric alternatives remain available as labeled options. Native angles, including 24-degree Quindecile, remain unchanged.

The earlier geometric forms for the four common minor aspects are retained. The acceptance is recorded here and in [decisions.json](decisions.json), preserving the earlier review packets byte-for-byte. No earlier glyph metadata, source, font binary or report is rewritten.

## New proposals in this packet

The current Lilith, Fortune, Spirit, angle-label and motion-label studies remain drafts for owner review. In particular, circled S for Spirit is proposed; no universal Spirit character is asserted. The diamond-on-cross Lilith study is conditional on model compatibility and is not a mean/true style toggle.

Existing S, D and V geometry is reused read-only inside the new label compositions. These are ordinary letters; their meanings come from the labeled context. The new packet does not implement a font feature, allocate a private-use value, change a calculation or select a computational model.
