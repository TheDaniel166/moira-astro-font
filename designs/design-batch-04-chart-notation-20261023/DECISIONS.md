# Accepted direction and new review proposals

23 October 2026

## Spirit: accepted by the owner

The owner selected **circled S as the default** for the Lot of Spirit and **circled vertical bar as its alternate**. The word-shaped Spirit artwork stays withdrawn from the glyph options. The ordinary descriptive name, semantic identifier `lot.spirit`, plain-text fallback and accessibility label remain available.

This accepts the exact existing Batch 03 drawings `spirit` and `spirit-vertical`. Neither is redrawn. [decisions.json](decisions.json) binds the choice to their source registry and individual drawing hashes, retained in the [frozen source forms](references/prior-point-forms.json).

U+24C8 CIRCLED LATIN CAPITAL LETTER S remains the documented contextual mapping candidate for the default; U+29B6 CIRCLED VERTICAL BAR remains the alternate candidate. Neither is a dedicated LOT OF SPIRIT character. This task records the design choice; it does not implement a cmap, feature access, normalization policy or new font build.

This later decision supersedes the earlier provisional Spirit review status without editing the earlier packets. It does not imply approval of every other Batch 03 drawing. The previously accepted minor-aspect letter family and its retained alternates continue unchanged.

## Batch 04: chart notation proposals

This batch follows the inventory's remaining P1 numeral/punctuation/spacing list. Twenty-seven character entries receive proposed geometry or spacing: ten digits, fifteen visible unit/punctuation characters and two blank spaces. A slashed-zero alternate adds one form study without adding a character. The slash uses the existing Batch 03 component unchanged.

The proposed numerals are lining and tabular, with 680-unit advances in these proofs. The unslashed zero is the proposed default. These choices and all new drawings remain drafts for owner review.

The remaining Latin support and later P2 symbol families stay on the existing roadmap. Completing these notation studies does not complete P1 font coverage. No private-use values, OpenType feature slots or distribution license are assigned.
