# Accepted zero direction and Latin review scope

23 October 2026

## Zero: accepted by the owner

The owner selected **unslashed zero as the default**, with **slashed zero as an alternate**. Both retain U+0030 DIGIT ZERO. The accepted geometry is the exact Batch 04 artwork; neither outline is redrawn.

[decisions.json](decisions.json) records the source registry hash and separate drawing hashes for `notation.u0030` and `zero-slashed`. These accepted choices supersede the earlier proposed default/alternate status while preserving the old packet as review history. No feature slot, font mapping or keyboard access is implemented here, and this choice does not imply approval of every other Batch 04 drawing.

## Existing accepted choices

- The minor-aspect letter family remains the default, with its earlier alternates retained.
- Circled S remains the accepted Spirit default, with circled vertical bar retained as the alternate. The word-shaped Spirit artwork remains withdrawn.
- Existing glyph geometry, corrected Capricorn and font binaries are preserved.

## Batch 05: 31 new ordinary letters

The complete Basic Latin notation alphabet is A-Z and a-z: 52 characters. Twenty-one standalone drawings already exist and serve as frozen controls in this packet. The remaining **13 uppercase and 18 lowercase letters** are newly drawn:

| New uppercase | New lowercase |
|---|---|
| B F G H J K L O P T W Y Z | b d f g h j k l m n o q s u v w y z |

The reused uppercase letters are A C D E I M N Q R S U V X; the reused lowercase letters are a c e i p r t x. Their contours are unchanged. Q, N and U retain their exact Batch 02 paths and receive provisional translation/advance values for text composition. The Batch 03 component drawings and advances are copied unchanged.

The compact Batch 02 **Qd** is a contextual composite rather than a standalone d master. It remains intact. The new full-size d completes the ordinary alphabet; a comparison specimen keeps both compositions visible without implementing a ligature or replacing the compact source.

The new letterforms, including single-storey g and footed l, remain owner-review proposals. This batch completes drawings for the 52 basic letters; it does not provide full Latin-script coverage or integrate them into the font. Accented letters, combining marks, Greek coordinate letters, final shaping and public release remain separate work.
