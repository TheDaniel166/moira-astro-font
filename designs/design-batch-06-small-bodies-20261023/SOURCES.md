# Sources and attribution roles

Accessed 23 October 2026. Unicode assignments are pinned to 17.0.0.

| ID | Source | Evidence role and limits |
|---|---|---|
| `unicode17` | [UnicodeData](https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/UnicodeData.txt), [frozen records](references/unicode17-records.json) | Formal assigned character identities. Does not define a universal astrological convention or ephemeris |
| `unicode17-arrows` | [Retained Unicode 17 chart](references/Unicode17-Miscellaneous-Symbols-Arrows.pdf), grid page 2 and [details](references/unicode17-astraea-hygiea-nessus-detail.png), [Eris/Sedna detail](references/unicode17-eris-sedna-detail.png) | Final U+2BD9, U+2BDA, U+2BDC and U+2BF0-U+2BF2 structures; no contour extraction |
| `unicode17-alchemical` | [Official archived chart](https://www.unicode.org/charts/PDF/Unicode-17.0/U170-1F700.pdf#page=5), [retained PDF](references/Unicode17-Alchemical-Symbols.pdf), [detail](references/unicode17-astronomical-forms-detail.png) | Final historical alternatives and U+1F77B-U+1F77F assignments. The block title does not change their astronomical character identities |
| `faulks2016` | [David Faulks, L2/16-080](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2016/16080-add-astrology.pdf), sections 5-6; [PDF](references/Faulks-2016-astrology-proposal.pdf) | Modern Astraea/Hygiea usage and Nessus structure/attribution report. Tentative positions, uncertain authorship and popularity claims are not adopted as final authority |
| `bala-miller2023` | [Gavin Jared Bala and Kirk Miller, L2/23-207](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2023/23207-historical-asteroids.pdf), pp.5-7 and illustrated sources; [PDF](references/Bala-Miller-2023-historical-asteroids.pdf) | Historical inverted-anchor and serpent/star forms, differentiated from modern astrological forms. No independent full historical collation is claimed |
| `faulks-eris2016` | [David Faulks, L2/16-173](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2016/16173-eris-sedna.pdf), section 3; [PDF](references/Faulks-2016-Eris-Sedna.pdf) | Competing Eris forms, Seltzer attribution and Sedna usage. The proposal's original codepoint positions are obsolete |
| `miller2021` | [Kirk Miller, L2/21-224](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2021/21224-dwarf-planet-syms.pdf); [PDF](references/Miller-2021-outer-body-symbols.pdf) | Usage and structures for Haumea/Makemake/Gonggong/Quaoar/Orcus. The proposal's taxonomy discussion and broad usage opinions are not font semantics |
| `moskowitz` | [Denis Moskowitz, Symbols for large trans-Neptunian objects](https://www.suberic.net/~dmm/astro/tno.html) | Creator's concept explanations, public-domain offer and attribution request; Makemake collaboration with John T. Whelan. His orbital/taxonomic statements are not adopted as a model authority |
| `unicode-blog2022` | [Deborah Anderson, Unicode Consortium account](https://blog.unicode.org/2022/05/out-of-this-world-new-astronomy-symbols.html) | Encoding context and named symbol creators. Does not establish traditional cultural status or validate astrological interpretation |
| `jpl-identity` | [NASA/JPL SBDB API documentation](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/sbdb.html), [ten retained responses](references/jpl-identities.json) | Independent current body-number/name and returned SPK-ID evidence only. Orbit output suppressed; no glyph authority, computation or native-catalog migration |
| `inventory` | [Frozen master-plan entries](references/planned-small-bodies.json) | Stable semantic IDs, scope, proposed defaults and older native-catalog ID fields |

## Moira artwork controls

[Earlier small-body drawings](references/prior-small-body-forms.json) preserve source hashes and unchanged Ceres/Pallas/Juno/Vesta/Chiron/Pholus geometry. [Core controls](references/prior-core-controls.json), [point forms](references/prior-point-forms.json), [Latin components](references/prior-letter-components.json) and [notation](references/prior-notation.json) retain the remaining comparison sources. These are original Moira artwork inputs; external symbols are consulted visually as structural references.

The [retrieval manifest](references/document-retrieval.json) records URLs and hashes for the four newly retrieved PDFs. The other two documentary PDFs are frozen copies from Batch 01. [Unicode's retained license](references/UNICODE-LICENSE.txt) accompanies its data. Documentary notices and the underlying concept attributions remain separate from Moira's newly authored outlines and undecided distribution license.
