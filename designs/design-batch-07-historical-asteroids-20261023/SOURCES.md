The proposal credits its Flora and Parthenope lyre reference drawings to Moskowitz. Those historical symbol concepts and modern reference drawings are distinct from the new Moira coordinates. No inventor claim is made for Moira's underlying historical signs. The author page contains older historical assertions; the final Unicode chart and evidence in the proposal govern this packet's mapping and form decisions.

Unicode charts explicitly allow visual variation in font designs. They are used to identify characters and structural distinctions. Their reference-font contours are not extracted, copied or traced. All 19 Moira drawings are newly authored paths and geometric primitives in `build_designs.py`; earlier Moira controls are copied intact.

The historical material was reviewed through the proposal's reproduced evidence, including Hind's 1852 table (Figure 26) and the Thetis, Proserpina, Bellona and Leukothea announcements (Figures 21–24). This is a bounded source review, not independent collation of every original publication.
