# Design and review decisions

23 October 2026

The user acknowledged the Irene/lyre refinement and requested continued minor-body symbol work. This packet continues with seven extended centaurs already in the P3 inventory. The latest Irene/lyre candidates are preserved as the current revision checkpoint; no font integration or publication is inferred.

Seven primary candidates and three alternatives are supplied for visual review. The choices favor distinct head shapes and open space at chart sizes while retaining the source conventions. Hylonome's fork, Thereus's T-head and Bienor's beta-like head remain proposals, not owner-approved defaults.

## Visual review focus

- Chariklo's open C, Bienor's two lobes and the unchanged Pholus single lobe must remain distinguishable.
- Asbolus's straight bar and Thereus's bowed crosspiece must remain distinct.
- The TF monogram is retained without an oval. It does not replace ordinary T or F text.
- The Elatus arrowhead is separated from the C endpoints to reduce crowding.

Light and dark specimens include enlarged forms, alternative pairs, repeated 14/18/24/32 px em samples and synthetic chart rows. At the smallest size, antialiasing still affects fine head detail. No final font-rasterization or hinting claim is made.

## Carried preferences

- Minor aspects: letter family default with retained alternatives.
- Spirit: circled S default, circled vertical bar alternative.
- Zero: unslashed default, slashed alternative.
- Preserve existing Capricorn, glyphs, Latin/notation components, font binaries and unrelated research.

## Access decision

All seven mappings remain unresolved. No Unicode value, private-use assignment, stylistic-set slot or automatic text substitution is allocated. Readable body names and stable semantic IDs remain explicit. Outline approval and encoding access can be addressed after this design checkpoint.
