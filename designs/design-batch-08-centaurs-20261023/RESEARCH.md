## Attribution and uncertainty

Stein attributes Chariklo's selected convention to Piotr Piotrowski and Asbolus's to Robert von Heeren. He includes Cyllarus among the symbols he designed. The reviewed material does not establish the original designers of our selected Hylonome, Elatus, Thereus and Bienor forms. They are recorded as displayed conventions with authorship unconfirmed.

The collaborative Hylonome attribution on the provisional page applies to its DW monogram, which is not drawn in this packet. It is not transferred to the fork or crossed-arm forms. The chart's Bienor update establishes a displayed variant, not proof that its compiler invented it.

## Mapping boundary

The existing unresolved mappings are retained: empty `codepoints`, null `pua_assignment`, ordinary name fallback. This states that no mapping is selected here; it is not a proof of absence from all Unicode. Neither source-font key positions nor visually similar Latin/Greek characters are adopted as body characters.
