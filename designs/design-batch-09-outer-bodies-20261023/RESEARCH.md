## Form evidence

[Moskowitz's own notes](https://www.suberic.net/~dmm/astro/tno.html) establish Varuna's va/lasso construction, the Iota/Xi wheel adaptation and both hippocamps. [Stein's Ixion page](https://www.zanestein.com/Ixion.htm) attributes the Latin wheel to Sandy Turnbull. [Stein's Varda section](https://www.zanestein.com/Trans_Pluto.htm) labels its star as his symbol. The [form chart](https://zanestein.com/CentaursFontGlyphs.pdf), PDF page 3, also displays Varda and V/water Varuna; the latter's original designer remains unconfirmed.

These are modern attested conventions. Varuna's script inspiration does not make its body symbol a traditional astronomical character. The selected outlines are original Moira constructions. Source uncertainty and competing forms remain explicit.
