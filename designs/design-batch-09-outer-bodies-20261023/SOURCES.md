Moskowitz's concepts retain credit. The Latin Salacia form credits his collaboration with Kirk Miller; the Latin Ixion form credits Sandy Turnbull. The V/water Varuna designer is unconfirmed. Varda's star convention is attributed through Stein's own image label and chart.

Moskowitz offers his symbols to the public domain while requesting credit. This packet credits the concepts and authors separately from the new Moira coordinates. No reference image or third-party font outline is imported, extracted or traced. The Moira font distribution policy remains unchanged.

The intact source documents and enlarged browser/PDF captures are internal research references. The public review sheets contain only original Moira drawings and unchanged earlier Moira comparison controls.
