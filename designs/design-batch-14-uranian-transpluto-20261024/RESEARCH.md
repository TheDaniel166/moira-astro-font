# Batch 14 research: Hamburg/Uranian symbols and Transpluto

24 October 2026. Nine original Moira SVG studies for visual review. Eight belong to the Hamburg/Uranian hypothetical-point family; Transpluto remains a separate convention.

## Reference forms and identities

The final [Unicode 17 chart](https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/charts/PDF/U2B00.pdf) and frozen character data establish the assigned identities. [David Faulks, L2/16-064R](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2016/16064r-uranian-planets.pdf), p. 1, supplies a labelled reference for the eight-point set; pp. 3-8 supply chart and text examples. The PDF was rendered for inspection: extracted text substitutes misleading digits or unrelated symbols for its embedded glyphs. These documents supply recognition evidence, not Moira outlines or an orbital model.

Reference glyph shapes are not prescriptive contours. The new paths use Moira's own proportions, round caps/joins and stroke language, with no external font data extracted or traced.

## Historical witness for the first four

[Michael Feist, The Hamburg School: The Early Years](https://uraniansociety.com/USIG_articles/article_history_of_uranian_astrology_michael_feist.pdf), printed p. 13 (PDF page 6), reproduces German 1935 and English 1939 rulebook material, both labelled p. 42. The German example shows the first four symbols; the English example uses name initials. This is useful evidence of form and label variation. It is a reproduction in a later tradition account, not a directly inspected original 1935 volume or physical validation of the points.

No dates of physical discovery, population claims, astrological effects or historical glyph-inventor claims are adopted by these studies. The source's wider historical/physical narrative is outside the design evidence used here.

## Transpluto evidence and variant

[L2/16-080, Additional Symbols for Astrology](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2016/16080-add-astrology.pdf), pp. 3-5, documents Transpluto with an upward arrow and a divided ring. Its p. 3 heading shows curved-bowl and straight-divider forms. The curved-bowl form is selected here, following the final chart; the straight variant remains in the frozen reference pages for later review. No tenth Moira master is introduced.

The proposal's draft **U+2BE8** is not the final assignment. This packet records **U+2BD7 TRANSPLUTO** from Unicode 17. The proposal does not establish a definitive symbol inventor, so none is asserted. Its possible alternate names are not made automatic aliases to other Moira point/model records.

## Identity boundaries

The symbols label conventional hypothetical-point identities. They do not select an orbit, ephemeris, calculation model or physical-body admission. In particular:

- Hypothetical Cupido remains separate from physical **(763) Cupido** and similarly named lots. No asteroid Cupido icon is invented here.
- Moira's **Vulkanus** display label retains Unicode's exact **VULCANUS** character name. Intramercurial **Vulcan** remains a different research identity.
- Transpluto is separate from the eight Hamburg points and from Pluto's existing assigned alternate forms.
- Poseidon keeps its own assigned identity. Similarity to Pisces or an unrelated script letter does not justify a character alias.

The label-boundary specimen uses ordinary Moira letters/numerals for the unresolved physical/research records, without presenting those labels as newly designed pictographs.

## Optical choices

All nine use 60-unit source strokes and 1,000-unit source proof cells. Kronos keeps a visible gap beneath its cap. Cupido separates the shared bar from the lower crossbar. Apollon opens both windows; Admetos preserves four ring fields. Zeus terminates its upright at the crossed-arm junction, while Vulkanus joins an arrow to a triangle. Transpluto retains two unfilled fields.

The preview uses the established 1.08 scale and y offset -50 in a 1,000-unit em. It supplies light/inverse 14/18/24/32 px proofs, existing-glyph comparisons, named chart rows and explicit identity boundaries. Values are invented for typography review. Source cells and SVG raster captures are not final font advances, hinting or production-chart certification.

## Source limitations and preservation

The publisher's older ephemeris-sample URL was unavailable through the web tool and is not used as inspected evidence. The version 17 chart is a frozen copy from the earlier design research, with its source hash retained. Feist and both proposal PDFs were fetched and rendered for this packet; source receipts identify their scope.

Only the nine original Moira masters are candidate future glyph assets. Reference PDFs/renders remain local research material. Every older glyph, font, historical variant, master inventory and research packet is outside this task's writes.
