# Batch 15 design decisions

25 October 2026 · Draft 01 · Four proposed original outlines

| Identity | Chosen construction | Optical emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| (1181) Lilith | Four-finger hand and angled thumb | Three open finger slots, clear palm and thumb bend |
| (763) Cupido | C-shaped shield around Venus | Open ring and shield spacing; lower cross below the shield |
| (433) Eros | Pierced heart with down-left arrow tip | Open heart cleft, two fields and an exterior arrowhead |
| (128) Nemesis | Two uprights, arched upper field and angular lower bowl | Two fields, wide crossbar and flat lower rim |

The outlines are original Moira geometry following attributed/documented constructions. They do not assert that Moira invented those symbol concepts. The source reference for Nemesis also retains the rounder ending; no extra alternate master is introduced in this four-form pilot.

Use 60-unit strokes with round caps and joins in 1,000-unit source proof cells. The established preview scale remains 1.08. These are not final font advances. Existing letter/number/unit and body-symbol controls retain their own source paths and advances.

Keep permanent numbers in the registry and in unfamiliar-symbol labels. The four physical records remain distinct from lunar-apogee Lilith, Hamburg Cupido, similarly named lots and hypothetical/fictional Nemesis identities. Native and current JPL identifier fields stay separate.

Every character, Unicode name/codepoint, PUA, token and feature assignment remains null. In particular, do not repurpose U+2BE0 CUPIDO, U+26B8 BLACK MOON LILITH, heart emoji, or the legacy Centaur-font keyboard characters. Later integration requires an explicit access/encoding decision and new binary/shaping checks. The current 0.6 font remains unchanged.

The master inventory, original source packets, historical variants, native models and unrelated research are preserved. User approval of these drawings is separate from a future font integration task.
