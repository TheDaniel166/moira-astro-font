The source PDF render includes the entire supplied reference pages. Detail crops are analytical aids only. No reference font is installed, copied into a master or used to extract outline coordinates. Vendor and documentary notices remain intact.

Search also surfaced secondary community pages and stock-art listings. They were not used as outline inputs or adopted as primary attribution evidence. The unavailable old Zane Stein ephemeris page was not treated as inspected material.
