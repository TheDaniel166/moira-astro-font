# Batch 16 design decisions

25 October 2026 · draft 01 · awaiting visual review

Eleven new semantic designs use the established Moira construction: 1,000-unit source cells, 60-unit round strokes, 1.08 preview scale and −50 vertical preview offset. Existing glyph drawings and advances are comparison controls only. The source SVGs remain editable stroke drawings; no contours are inserted into a TTF or WOFF2 in this packet.

## Pairs and families

- **Diu / Noc:** one solar-ring/horizon construction, reflected vertically. Both refer to a declared chart category. Neither changes the existing Sun or Moon identity.
- **Dom / Det:** one house outline; an interior point for domicile, an outward arrow for detriment. The arrowhead is separated from the wall so it does not create two tiny accidental enclosed wedges.
- **Exa / Fal:** a vertical arrow and a separate bar, reflected vertically. The arrow-to-bar clearance is 80 source units after stroke expansion. No degree of exaltation or scoring is implied.
- **Tri:** three linked solid nodes distinguish the proposal from a plain trine triangle. At very small sizes the node distinction weakens; the `Tri` label carries the category unambiguously.
- **Bnd / Fac:** a bounded interval and a three-field panel. These refer to different categories and do not specify a ruler table. The face metaphor is explicitly for this inventory's decan context.
- **Ant / C.Ant:** capped reflection axes with two unconnected rings; exact quarter-turn counterparts. The caps reduce resemblance to division and the unconnected rings differ from opposition. Small-size detail is still a review limit.

All eleven shapes are **Moira-original concepts**, rather than claims to reconstruct historical marks. The abbreviations are also proposed house style, not quoted standards. Full names remain the fallback. No pictogram, label or default has been approved merely by producing this packet.

## Review questions

The main design decision is whether each unfamiliar pictogram earns its place alongside its short label. Review the node triangle against trine, the reflection pair at 14/18 px, and the house/exit pair before choosing defaults. At 24/32 px the sampled counters and strokes survive in both themes, but sampled pixels do not establish recognition by an untrained reader.

No model or table choice belongs in these outlines. Horizon handling, sect policy, rulers, scores, coordinates and reflection results remain application data. The original inventory and current 0.7 build are preserved.
