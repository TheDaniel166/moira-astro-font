# Batch 16 research

25 October 2026 · eleven design proposals · no font integration

The reviewed sources support the meanings, but do not establish a standard set of eleven pictograms. The new marks are **Moira-original symbolic concepts and outlines**. Their short labels are proposed Moira abbreviations. This is a bounded finding about the inspected material, not a claim that no other notation exists.

## What the witnesses establish

[Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos I.7, Robbins translation](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/1b%2A.html) distinguishes diurnal and nocturnal planetary categories. Chart sect, a planet's own sect and a planet's in-sect status require separate data. The font does not infer any of them. The Sun/horizon picture is our schematic metaphor for a declared day/night chart category; the Moon's visibility is not its criterion.

[Lilly, digital reproduction](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Astrologia/Astro%20y%20Religion/Astro%20Lilly%2C%20William%20-%20Christian%20Astrology%20Vol%201%201647.pdf), printed pp.101–104, names house, exaltation, triplicity, term and face, with detriment and fall in the table. Here domicile corresponds to house, bound to term, and face to the decan context on p.103. We do not select rulers, scores or a universal meaning for every occurrence of “face.”

Printed pp.90–92 describe solstitial reflection and its opposite. Lilly's example maps 10° Taurus to 20° Leo; his Saturn example maps 20°35′ Leo to 9°25′ Taurus and then 9°25′ Scorpio. The marks illustrate these relations, rather than replacing an ordinary aspect glyph.

The PDF's filename and added cover say 1647, while its internal title says second edition 1659. It is a retyped digital reproduction, not a verified original facsimile. Poppler reports unavailable Symbol/ArialUnicode fonts; readable prose supplies the cited relation evidence. No claim about an absent pictogram rests on a missing font.

The independently inspected [Skyscript table image](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/im/dignities2.gif), linked from its [essential-dignities page](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/essential_dignities.html), uses modern typeset category headings. We use it as a heading witness, not an original facsimile or an adopted ruler table.

## Proposed design vocabulary

| Category | Moira pictogram proposal | Proposed label |
|---|---|---|
| Diurnal sect | Solar ring above a horizon | Diu |
| Nocturnal sect | The same solar form below the horizon | Noc |
| Domicile | Point inside a house | Dom |
| Exaltation | Upward arrow above a lower bar | Exa |
| Triplicity | Three linked solid nodes | Tri |
| Bound | Bracketed interval with a central division | Bnd |
| Face | Three open decan fields in one panel | Fac |
| Detriment | House with an outward arrow | Det |
| Fall | Downward arrow below an upper bar | Fal |
| Antiscion | Two rings beside a capped vertical mirror axis | Ant |
| Contra-antiscion | The quarter-turned counterpart | C.Ant |

The mirror-axis caps help distinguish contra-antiscion from division. The triplicity nodes help distinguish it from trine. These are design devices, not claims of historical attestation. At 14 px those details are weak: retain a label or legend rather than relying on an unfamiliar mark alone.

## Reflection illustration

In a fixed schematic with Aries 0° at right and Cancer 90° at top:

- antiscion longitude = `(180° − longitude) mod 360°`;
- contra-antiscion longitude = `(−longitude) mod 360°`.

The first reflects across the solstitial axis; the second across the equinoctial axis. At 40° longitude the illustrations produce 140° and 320°. The 320° counterpart is derived here; it is not presented as a second quotation from Lilly. Axis orientation is a diagram convention, not a requirement to rotate charts or glyphs with a chart wheel. The local example checks validate this explanatory diagram, not the engine.
