# Batch 17 design decisions

25 October 2026 · draft 01 · visual approval pending

Six proposed pictograms follow the existing Moira construction: 1,000-unit source cells, 60-unit round strokes, 1.08 preview scale and a −50 vertical preview offset. Solid markers use 90-unit radii, large enough to distinguish knots from thin stems in chart proofs. Existing source drawings and advances remain untouched.

## Modality family

Cardinal uses a solid origin point and a horizontal arrow; Fixed holds a solid point between upright stops; Mutable bridges two solid points with a bending stroke. This is an original visual vocabulary for beginning, holding and transition. These metaphors are not assertions that an old author used the drawings. No glyph is assigned to a personality trait, a direction of physical motion or a zodiac frame.

The [comparison sheet](family-comparisons-light.html) places Cardinal beside Mars and exaltation; Fixed beside H and Pisces; Mutable beside opposition and Mercury. The central knot differentiates Fixed from H, while Mutable uses solid points and a curved bridge rather than open opposition rings. Distinct geometry does not establish unaided recognition, especially at 14 px.

## House family

The same open corner frame appears in Angular, Succedent and Cadent. A single solid marker occupies the corner, middle or terminal of its base. Source positions are `(250,760)`, `(525,760)` and `(800,760)`. The marker is visibly present at only the selected station, including below the thin frame. The common frame and all three marker selections are checked independently.

The frame is a mnemonic for a numbered triplet, repeated through houses 1–12. It is not a chart wheel or a picture of sky motion. No clockwise/anticlockwise chart orientation, equal-house geometry, angular-distance threshold or strength score is encoded. House category is kept distinct from domicile, face, fall and detriment in the comparison sheet.

## Text and access

Proposed companions are `Card`, `Fix`, `Mut`, `Ang`, `Suc`, `Cad`. `Card` and `Cad` do not share the same first three letters. These are normal existing Latin forms and source advances, not six extra masters, new ligatures or automatic substitutions.

All six pictograms remain **Moira-original proposals**. Full-name fallbacks, source records, P4 priority and text-first defaults are preserved. Unicode scalar, PUA, token and feature allocations remain null. No default, font mapping or model is selected by creating these studies.

## What to judge

Check whether the three modality metaphors are memorable beside their labels. For the house family, compare the dot positions at 14/18/24/32 px rather than relying on the enlarged cards. Fixed's central knot and the house stations need labels/legends at the smallest size. Approval of a concept is separate from later font integration and encoding checks.
