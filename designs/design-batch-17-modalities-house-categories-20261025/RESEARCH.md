# Batch 17 research

25 October 2026 · six original concept studies · no font integration

The reviewed material establishes the category meanings and memberships. It does not supply a single attested set of these six pictograms. The new shapes and short labels are **Moira-original proposals**. That is a finding about these inspected witnesses, not a claim that no other notation exists.

## Historical vocabulary and present labels

[Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos I.11, Robbins translation](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/1b%2A.html), distinguishes solstitial, equinoctial, solid and bicorporeal groups. His first distinction is not simply three categories under today's names. The modern cardinal set combines his solstitial and equinoctial groups; the fixed and mutable sets correspond to his solid and bicorporeal memberships. The webpage's modern editorial explanation is separate from the translated ancient text.

[Lilly's retyped digital reproduction](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Astrologia/Astro%20y%20Religion/Astro%20Lilly%2C%20William%20-%20Christian%20Astrology%20Vol%201%201647.pdf), Book I Ch.XVI, physical PDF p.112 (printed 88), gives moveable/cardinal, fixed and common groups. Book I Ch.VI, physical p.72, gives the three numbered-house groups. Only those classifications are used; the surrounding seasonal, personal and strength interpretations are not requirements for these glyphs.

[Deborah Houlding's authored STA teaching sheet](https://sta.co/portal/pluginfile.php/1745/mod_glossary/entry/413/aspects_STA.pdf), 2021, p.1, explicitly pairs cardinal/moveable and mutable/common. Its sign groups and labelled quadruplicity diagram corroborate the vocabulary crosswalk. The coloured legend keys and rotated square diagrams are teaching devices, not six category glyphs to be copied into Moira.

These are two different classifications. Their common grouping into three sets of four does not establish an automatic one-to-one equivalence. The specimen does not assign points, calculate house membership, choose a zodiac frame, or equate a numbered angular house with a specified distance from ASC/MC in every house system.

## Notation routes considered

The primary texts use category words; the STA sheet adds existing sign symbols and a labelled diagram. These support readable full names and label companions. A search also surfaced the sulfur/salt/Mercury analogy in a [secondary symbol compilation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrological_symbols). That is retained as a research lead, not a primary attestation for this project. It supplies neither the adopted outlines nor an assignment of the existing planetary Mercury character to Mutable.

Astrodienst introductory/wiki pages were also found, but direct opens returned a browser challenge. No claimed image inspection or design decision rests on those inaccessible pages or search thumbnails. No third-party font contours or reference illustrations were traced.

## New visual proposals

| Category | Moira-original concept | Proposed text companion |
|---|---|---|
| Cardinal | Solid origin point leading into an arrow | Card |
| Fixed | Solid point held between two upright stops | Fix |
| Mutable | Two solid points joined by a bending bridge | Mut |
| Angular | Marker at the corner of an open frame | Ang |
| Succedent | Marker at the middle of that frame's base | Suc |
| Cadent | Marker at the terminal of that frame's base | Cad |

The modality metaphors suggest beginning, holding and transition; they are our design rationale, not historical glyph reconstructions or personality claims. The house motif encodes first, second and third stations of numbered-house triplets. Its left-to-right reading order is a graphic convention, not a diagram of diurnal motion, cusps or equal house sizes.

`Card` and `Cad` deliberately retain different spellings. All companions compose existing Latin outlines and keep ordinary text semantics. Neither defaults nor character access are selected. The source inventory's text-first defaults, P4 priorities and blockers remain verbatim in [the brief](BATCH_BRIEF.json).

At 14 px the Fixed mark's central knot and the house markers are subtle. Keep a full name, companion or legend while readers learn the vocabulary. The [chart-size proof](chart-size-proof-light.html), [inverse proof](chart-size-proof-dark.html) and [comparisons](family-comparisons-light.html) make that decision reviewable.
