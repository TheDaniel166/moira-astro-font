# Batch 19 research

25 October 2026. Evidence is separated into naming, angle identity and visual form. A source using a word or fraction does not establish a universal glyph.

## Tao, 165°

**Tao is the selected primary name for this study.** The authored Planet Figures discussion calls the 165° side of its Telescope configuration tao, providing a contemporary witness for the requested name. This packet makes no claim about the name's inventor or earliest appearance. [Krap, Planet Figures](https://astrologyplanetfigures.wordpress.com/).

Other conventions call the same angle Quindecile or Contraquindecile. The source labels remain qualified aliases: Astro Gold uses Quindecile with lowercase q; Aureas uses Contraquindecile. Fei Cochrane's primer explicitly pairs 165° with 11/24 and illustrates an angular hooked form. [Astro Gold](https://www.astrogold.io/AG-MacOS-Help/aspects.html), [Aureas](https://www.aureas-astrology.com/contents/en-us/d1_News.html), [Cochrane, physical PDF p5](https://www.astrosoftware.com/Pub/Tech/FeisBlog/BasicAlphabetOfAstrology.pdf).

**Ta is new Moira notation**, composed from the existing T and a. It is not claimed to be a historical Tao sigil. The hooked form was inspected as a source witness but was not traced or adopted as a new alternate drawing.

The old inventory identifier `aspect.quindecile-165` remains only as an archival link to the unchanged planning record. The display name, specimen captions, fallback label and naming decision say Tao. This identifier does not create a font route or rename the existing native aspect.

## Hexadecile, 22.5°

The intended geometry is 360°/16 = 22.5°. Modern software sources explicitly use **Semioctile** at this angle; Astro Gold assigns contextual uppercase O. These establish the angle/alias crosswalk, not the proposed Hx abbreviation. [Astro Gold](https://www.astrogold.io/AG-MacOS-Help/aspects.html), [Aureas](https://www.aureas-astrology.com/contents/en-us/d1_News.html).

Wharton's collected works (1683), printed p92, names Hexadecile in astrological prose. The inspected paragraph does not give its angle or a unique glyph; adjacent pp91 and 93 were also inspected. This is evidence for the historical word, with the angle association kept separate. [Original scan, p92](https://books.google.com/books?id=LBroWVBBY2oC&pg=PA92).

There is a correction to the earlier Batch 02 notes: in Suignard's N4811 Table 1, Hexadecile appears in the **statistics** column at 1/16; the astrology cell is blank. That table must not be cited as astrological attestation for the word. The old packet is preserved, and this correction is recorded here. [N4811, physical p2](https://www.unicode.org/wg2/docs/n4811-fdbk-extra-aspect-sym.pdf).

**Hx** remains an explicit Moira study label using the inventory term. No unique traditional Hexadecile glyph is established by the inspected evidence.

## Seminovile, 20°

N4811 lists Seminovile in the astrology column at 1/18, which gives 20°. Both software manuals independently use Seminovile at 20°, and Astro Gold uses lowercase n in its own charts. [N4811, physical p2](https://www.unicode.org/wg2/docs/n4811-fdbk-extra-aspect-sym.pdf), [Astro Gold](https://www.astrogold.io/AG-MacOS-Help/aspects.html), [Aureas](https://www.aureas-astrology.com/contents/en-us/d1_News.html).

**Sn** is the Moira composition proposal. The existing N for Novile (40°) and raised N² for Binovile (80°) remain unchanged. Sn keeps these family members visually separate.

## Untion, 15°

N4811 lists Untion in the astrology column at 1/24, giving 15°. The other inspected sources use Semiduodecile or Quartosextile for this angle. This packet retains the inventory term Untion without claiming that it is the most common name or that a unique historical glyph has been established. [N4811, physical p2](https://www.unicode.org/wg2/docs/n4811-fdbk-extra-aspect-sym.pdf), [Astro Gold](https://www.astrogold.io/AG-MacOS-Help/aspects.html), [Aureas](https://www.aureas-astrology.com/contents/en-us/d1_News.html).

**Ut** is new Moira notation, distinct from existing U for Undecile and D for Decile. Lowercase d is retained only as the Astro Gold context alternative.

## Preservation and limits

The preserved Quindecile is 24° = 360°/15. Its Qd drawing and exact `[astro:quindecile]` route were checked in both current font binaries. Tao is 165° = 11/24 of a circle. They are separate entries with separate meanings.

The four studies remain P4 proposals. This work introduces no native aspect, orb, applying/separating rule, interpretation, calculated chart position or empirical validity claim. Ordinary q/O/n/d are not globally remapped. No code point is inferred from an old proposal document.

Noel Tyl's direct/archived pages were inaccessible through the research tools. A Paul Wade PDF address redirected to unrelated content, so cached search text was excluded. The unresolved historical questions remain explicit; none are filled with inferred artwork. See [source receipts and access notes](SOURCES.md).
