# Batch 20 design decisions

25 October 2026 · Three proposals, awaiting owner review

| Point | Drawing decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| East Point | Circled cross with a split arm, drawn with a 240-unit radius and 64-unit round strokes | Retains the observed modern form while fitting Moira's visual language |
| West Point | Exact 180° rotation of our EP drawing about the cell center | Expresses the selected opposite relationship as a coherent original pair |
| Polar Ascendant | Two open curved sides crossed by a rising stroke, with a solid knot at the right intersection | Gives the qualified point its own nonletter identity and a selected-intersection cue |

The Polar Ascendant study was refined during review: a closed loop resembled a slashed zero at small sizes. The final source has 220-unit top and bottom centerline gaps. Those openings are part of the identity, not missing contours. Comparisons include the existing unslashed zero, Earth, celestial/ecliptic poles, Vertex and anti-Vertex. The knot is a schematic cue; its page-right position and crossing angle do not describe physical chart geometry.

The common 1000-unit cell and advance follow the established source design convention. Proofs apply the same inherited 1.08 display scale and -50-unit vertical offset as the comparison family. Listed sizes are CSS/SVG em sizes, not the ink bounding-box height. No earlier drawing is rescaled in its source record or edited.

The East/West pair remains distinguishable by the split-arm side. At 14 px, preserve a legend; 18–32 px communicates the internal detail more comfortably. The Polar knot and openings are clearer at larger chart sizes. This is visual review evidence from local Chromium, not a measured user-recognition study.

EP/WP/PA are optional text labels, never substitutes for these three masters. Full names and qualified definitions remain accessible text. `definition_id`, `frame`, `equinox_or_epoch` and `branch_convention` are required metadata for calculated use. The font does not select the projection, coordinate frame or polar branch.

No Unicode/PUA, token, OpenType feature, default or font glyph name is allocated here. Existing inventory priorities remain P4; candidate records are unchanged. Integration, shaping/copy checks and font raster proofs belong to a later requested font build after visual review. Existing labels and historical variants remain untouched.
