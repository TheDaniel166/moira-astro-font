# Design decisions

Thirteen source masters cover ten concepts and three variants. All coordinates are original Moira work in 1000-unit cells using 64-unit round-ended strokes; filled crescent/diamond/disk regions supplement that stroke family. Proofs use the inherited 1.08 display scale and -50-unit vertical offset. Listed sizes are nominal em sizes, not ink height.

The four lunar marks deliberately form one family. The perigee crescent is outlined; interpolated apogee is filled. An extra bar, solid diamond or open basal bridge differentiates the proposed mean, osculating and interpolated identities. These are invented graphic cues, not stylistic substitutes for model metadata. Labels remain essential at 14 px. At 18–32 px the lower cues and outlined crescent counters are easier to inspect.

Proserpina retains the bracket-dot witness family and a ring alternative. The geometry is one drawn symbol, not assembled punctuation. Selena's plain reversed crescent and Arta's upward cradle remain visibly distinct from the perigee family. The rayed Selena is an original three-ray adaptation of the documented rayed family; its exact ray placement is not a facsimile of a source font.

The Waldemath emblem is explicitly invented: a solid disk, interrupted outer orbit and detached outward bar. It distinguishes this named hypothetical convention from the unchanged apogee Lilith and asteroid Lilith identities. Its appearance makes no physical-body or orbital-geometry claim.

Vulcan's fork/cross and downward-arrow forms follow author-published form witnesses. The fork is retained as the main review candidate because it is more individually recognizable than an ordinary arrow. Neither form selects Le Verrier, Weston or other elements. Existing Hamburg Vulkanus remains unchanged.

Ophiuchus follows its reference bowl/serpent family with independently drawn curves. It is a source-qualified modern convention symbol, not an addition to the existing zodiac model.

Proserpina, Selena, Arta and Ophiuchus have existing assigned reference identities verified against the copied Unicode 17.0 data. These are **candidate references only**. The drawing records allocate no characters, private-use values, entry tokens, OpenType features or defaults. The current font has not been rebuilt. Approval and later integration would require fresh shaping/copy checks and actual-font raster proofs.

The original master records and 204 comparison drawings retain their data unchanged. This packet does not change any earlier glyph, approved variant or research artifact.
