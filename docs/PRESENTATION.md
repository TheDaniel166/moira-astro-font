Start review at 18 px em for general symbols. Dense Fortuna, Makemake, Quaoar and fixed-star chart forms use 24 px in the reviewed examples. Historical fixed-star reconstructions generally use 32 px with the full name and witness. Fourteen pixels is a stress case. These are reviewed starting sizes, not universal perceptual guarantees.

Keep the historical fixed-star witness labels. Septile/Stationary, Decile/Direct and Fire/Trine require explicit aspect, motion or classification context. Shared mean/true-node and mean/osculating-apogee icons require model labels. The distinct lunar cues introduced in Batch 21 still do not select a calculation model.

All 27 satellite layouts use a 24 px parent symbol, a normal 14 px Roman designation, a 4 px internal gap and a shared baseline. Preserve the full satellite name and parent/designation in accessible labels and legends. These are composed layouts; they are not 27 standalone satellite emblems.

Measure the complete glyph/token advance, then reserve the label width and a 6 px gap. Wrap a whole entry when it does not fit. Lot abbreviations, historical sigils and satellite compositions can exceed a one-em cell. The complete-chart pilot will test the policies under crowded placements.
